Curriculum Focus

What Your Child Will Learn

  • Foundation of bead representation and finger manipulation techniques.
  • Rapid addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division on the physical abacus.
  • Mental abacus visualization enabling lightning-quick calculations without paper.
  • Pattern recognition and photographic memory enhancements.

How This Program Helps Your Child Grow
